Frequently asked questions

What is Syntrak?

Syntrak is a smart thermal suit for skiers with built-in motion capture, live AI coaching and 3D replay. Made by Syntrak Limited, a Hong Kong sports technology startup, it captures how your whole body moves on the mountain and turns every run into clear technique feedback for skiers and coaches.

Who founded Syntrak?

Syntrak was founded in Hong Kong in December 2025 by Matthew Lee, Founder and CEO. Matthew is a member of the Hong Kong National Ski Team, Hong Kong’s first SAHK-licensed ski coach and a Global Business and Marketing student at HKUST. His co-founders are Aidan Chan (Co-Founder and CFO), Adrian Yung (Co-Founder and Head of Performance), Hong Kong’s youngest two-time Winter Olympian, and Isaac Lee (Co-Founder and Head of Coaching), captain of the Hong Kong Ski Team.

Where is Syntrak based?

Syntrak is based in Hong Kong. The company is part of the Cyberport Incubation Programme and works out of HKUST InnoBay, the innovation hub at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology.

How does Syntrak work?

Put on the Syntrak suit, complete a quick calibration in the app and ski as you normally would. The suit captures your full-body movement and Syntrak turns it into run-level and turn-level insights. Get coaching cues while you ski, or review your 3D replay and AI coaching report after the run.

How is Syntrak different from a ski tracking app or smartwatch?

Ski tracking apps and smartwatches measure where you went: speed, distance and vertical. Syntrak measures how you skied. Capturing your whole body through every turn lets Syntrak show what your technique is doing and what to change next.

What does Syntrak’s AI coach do?

Syntrak’s AI coach turns your movement data into plain-language feedback: what happened in each turn, why it matters and what to work on next. It is built to adapt to each skier, so the advice reflects your own technique and progress rather than a generic checklist.

What is Syntrak’s 3D replay?

Syntrak’s 3D replay rebuilds your run as a moving 3D model of your body. Rewatch any turn from different angles and see exactly how you moved, instead of relying on memory, a score or someone filming from the side of the slope.

Who is Syntrak for?

Syntrak is for skiers who want to understand and improve their technique, from recreational skiers building consistency to racers who want structured performance analysis. Coaches and ski teams can use Syntrak to review their athletes’ runs and track progress over time.

Has Syntrak won any awards?

Yes. Syntrak was Champion (Gold Award, Tertiary Division) at YDC Dare to Change 2026 and won the Silver Award, People’s Choice Award and Best Elevator Pitch Award at the HKUST-SINO One Million Dollar Entrepreneurship Competition 2026. Syntrak was also a Startup World Cup Regional Finalist and an Asian Science Park Association Spotlight Asian Company.
