    How it works

    Put it on. Ski. See every movement.

    Step 1 of 3

    Sensors woven into your ski thermal

    Wear it as your base layer, under your usual ski gear. Hold a quick calibration pose and all sensors are ready.

    • Embeds sensors in the fabric

      The garment itself carries the sensors, for comprehensive performance insights.

    • Stays comfortable all day

      Premium thermal-grade fabric designed for all-day, high-output wear.

    • Keeps up with every run

      Track your runs without stopping.

    02

    Ski as you normally do

    No camera, no one filming you. The sensors record how your whole body moves, turn by turn, on every run.

    03

    Back inside, open any run in the Syntrak desktop app and see exactly what your body did.
